# Persona: The Design Leader in Transition

**ID:** persona-003
**Archetype:** VP/Director of Design managing a team through the AI shift
**Role:** VP of Product Design, CDO, or Design Director at a growth-stage company

## Context

She manages 8–25 designers. Her team is talented but structured around the traditional pipeline: researchers hand off to designers, designers hand off to engineers, engineers hand off to QA. Every quarter she watches intent degrade through the chain. Her team spends 30–50% of their capacity on coordination, not creation.

She's under pressure from leadership to "use AI" but the conversation is always about cost reduction, not capability expansion. She's seen the demos — generate a landing page in 60 seconds — and knows that's not transformation, that's automation of the wrong layer. She needs a framework that restructures how her team moves from vision to shipped product without destroying the team structure she's built.

She found Zero Vector through LinkedIn or a conference talk. The "for leaders" positioning caught her attention because it addressed her actual problem: pipeline compression, not headcount reduction.

## Goals

- Restructure the design-to-engineering pipeline to eliminate lossy handoffs
- Upskill her team into a model where designers can direct AI agents, not just produce static deliverables
- Maintain team cohesion and morale during the transition
- Demonstrate measurable velocity and quality improvements to justify the investment

## Frustrations

- "My designers draw pictures. My engineers interpret. We lose 50–70% of intent in the gap between Figma and code."
- "Leadership wants AI to replace headcount. I want it to amplify what my team can already do."
- "Every 'AI transformation' pitch I've seen is about speed. None of them are about craft."
- "I need a progression model — not everyone on my team will become a builder overnight, and that's okay."

## Quote

"I don't need my team to learn to code. I need them to learn to direct systems that code. That's a different skill."

## Behavioral Signals

- Reads Lenny's Newsletter, Reforge, and design leadership Substacks
- Attends DesignOps conferences
- Has a budget for team training and is evaluating frameworks
- Skeptical of vendor-driven "transformation" — wants methodology, not a platform
- Tracks cycle time and handoff metrics already
