# Persona: The Frustrated Designer

**ID:** persona-001
**Archetype:** Mid-career designer hitting the translation wall
**Role:** Senior Product Designer, 6–12 years experience

## Context

She's good at what she does. She runs workshops, synthesizes research, builds Figma prototypes that stakeholders love. But she's exhausted by the gap between what she designs and what ships. Every handoff loses signal. Every spec document becomes a game of telephone. She's watched features ship that barely resemble what she intended, and the worst part is nobody notices but her.

She's heard about AI-native design but doesn't know where to start. She's tried Cursor and v0 but felt like she was vibe-coding — generating output without understanding why it worked. She wants a path from "designer who hands off" to "designer who ships," but she's not willing to sacrifice craft for speed.

## Goals

- Ship products that match her original intent without degradation through handoff layers
- Learn enough code literacy to direct AI agents with specificity, not just prompts
- Maintain design rigor and craft while moving faster
- Stop spending 60% of her energy on translation and spend it on thinking

## Frustrations

- "I spend two weeks on a Figma prototype and the engineer interprets it in four hours. Half my decisions are invisible to them."
- "Every arrow in our pipeline is where we lose signal. Research → PM → Design → Spec → Dev → QA. Six handoffs. Six lossy translations."
- "AI tools let me generate UI but I can't tell if what it built is good or just fast."
- "I want to build in code but every tutorial assumes I want to become a software engineer. I don't. I want to remain a designer who can build."

## Quote

"Fall in love with the problem, not the solution. But right now I can't even get the solution to match the problem I fell in love with."

## Behavioral Signals

- Reads design Twitter/Bluesky but feels alienated by the "AI will replace designers" discourse
- Has a side project she's tried to build three times with different tools
- Subscribes to newsletters about design systems and methodology
- Skeptical of hype but genuinely curious about what's changed
