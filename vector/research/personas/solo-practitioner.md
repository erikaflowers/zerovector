# Persona: The Solo Practitioner

**ID:** persona-002
**Archetype:** Technical builder shipping real products alone
**Role:** Indie developer, founder, or senior engineer with design sensibility

## Context

He already codes. He might already design. He's built products before — maybe a SaaS tool, maybe an open-source library, maybe a client project. He's been using AI tools (Claude, Cursor, Copilot) for months and has seen genuine productivity gains, but he's also noticed the debt accumulating. The code works but the architecture is incoherent. The UI functions but has no intentionality. He's shipping faster but building worse.

He discovered Zero Vector through the manifesto or through Investiture. The idea that AI should be directed, not deferred to — that craft and velocity are not a tradeoff — resonated immediately. He wants a structured practice, not another tool.

## Goals

- Ship production-quality products as a one-person team with AI agents
- Maintain architectural coherence and design intentionality at speed
- Have a repeatable methodology that survives tool changes
- Build a portfolio of real shipped work, not side projects that stall

## Frustrations

- "I can generate a full-stack app in a weekend but it falls apart the moment I need to change something. There's no architecture, just output."
- "Vibe coding is fun until you have to maintain what you built."
- "I want AI to be my crew, not my crutch. But every tutorial treats it like autocomplete."
- "I'm fast but I'm not intentional. Speed without standards is just chaos with a deploy button."

## Quote

"If it's not in the repo, it doesn't exist in my workflow. Period."

## Behavioral Signals

- Active on GitHub, ships regularly
- Has tried multiple AI coding tools and formed opinions
- Reads long-form technical writing (not just tutorials)
- Values craft and is embarrassed by work that feels generated
- Looking for a system, not a shortcut
