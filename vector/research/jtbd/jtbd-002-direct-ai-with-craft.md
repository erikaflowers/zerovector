# JTBD-002: Direct AI with Craft

**Priority:** Critical
**Personas:** persona-001, persona-002, persona-004

## Job Statement

When I'm using AI tools to build products, I want to direct them with architectural intent and design judgment, so I can ship at AI speed without accumulating the debt that comes from unstructured generation.

## Current Alternatives

- Prompting AI with no structure — fast but produces incoherent output
- Detailed specifications before AI generation — slow, defeats the purpose
- Post-hoc review of AI output — doesn't scale, catches symptoms not causes
- Ignoring AI entirely — safe but increasingly uncompetitive

## Evidence

- Philosophy page: "Work in the medium" — build in the real material, not a representation
- Builders page: "Not replacing you — meeting you where you are and asking: what could you do if nothing was in the way?"
- Approach page: Five phases (Discover → Define → Invest → Build → Deliver) provide structured AI direction
- Investiture: The entire skill chain exists to give AI agents doctrine to follow
