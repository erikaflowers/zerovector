# JTBD-001: Ship Without Handoff

**Priority:** Critical
**Personas:** persona-001 (Frustrated Designer), persona-002 (Solo Practitioner), persona-003 (Design Leader)

## Job Statement

When I have a clear vision for a product or feature, I want to go from concept to shipped artifact without translation layers, so I can preserve the intent and craft that makes the work worth doing.

## Current Alternatives

- Traditional handoff pipeline (research → design → spec → eng → QA → deploy) — works but loses 30–70% of intent at each transition
- Vibe coding with AI (prompt → output → ship) — fast but architecturally incoherent and unmaintainable
- No-code tools (Webflow, Framer) — preserves some design intent but hits a ceiling on complexity
- Hiring a "full-stack designer" — rare, expensive, and the term means different things to different people

## Evidence

- Home page: "The distance between human intent and working product should be zero."
- Builders page: "Not vibe-coding. This is intentional creation at full velocity."
- For Hire page: "One designer, an AI crew, and the full pipeline."
- Multiple personas cite handoff degradation as their #1 frustration
