# JTBD-003: Restructure Team Pipeline

**Priority:** High
**Personas:** persona-003 (Design Leader), persona-004 (Technical Leader)

## Job Statement

When my team is spending 30–50% of their capacity on coordination instead of creation, I want to restructure the pipeline from sequential handoffs to parallel collaboration, so I can increase velocity without reducing headcount or quality.

## Current Alternatives

- DesignOps / process optimization — incremental, doesn't address the fundamental handoff structure
- Cross-functional squads — better than silos but still preserves the design → dev gap
- "Everyone learns code" mandates — demoralizing, ignores existing expertise
- AI automation of individual steps — speeds up each handoff but doesn't eliminate them

## Evidence

- Leaders page: "Pipeline Collapse: Traditional 8-step pipelines compress to 3-4 steps"
- Enterprise page: "30–50% of capacity consumed by coordination overhead"
- Approach page: Design and Build are the same act — not sequential phases
- Investiture: Crew skill decomposes work into parallel agent-scoped tasks
