# Assumption 004: Free Curriculum Builds Community Without Paywall

**Status:** hypothesis
**Risk:** Medium (Impact: High × Confidence: Medium)
**Personas:** persona-001, persona-002

## Statement

Open Vector — a free, open-source curriculum of 60+ lessons — will attract a self-selecting community of serious practitioners, and the community itself will generate demand for premium services (Arroyo Labs consulting, Investiture enterprise adoption) without needing a paywall, certification, or gated content.

## Why This Matters

The business model depends on free content driving organic growth → community → consulting/enterprise pipeline. If free content attracts tourists rather than builders, the funnel doesn't convert.

## Signals That Support It

- Community members completing multiple lessons (not just skimming)
- Organic inbound from content readers to Arroyo Labs / enterprise inquiries
- Community-contributed content, PRs, or research artifacts
- Repeat visitors and deepening engagement (not one-and-done)

## Signals That Would Invalidate It

- High traffic, low depth — visitors read the manifesto but don't enter the curriculum
- No conversion from content to consulting/enterprise pipeline within 6 months
- Community engagement stalls after initial launch spike
- Competitors paywall similar content and outperform on practitioner quality
