# Assumption 003: One Person + Agents = Former Small Team

**Status:** testing
**Risk:** High (Impact: High × Confidence: Medium)
**Personas:** persona-002, persona-003

## Statement

A single practitioner with AI agents, structured by Investiture's doctrine and skill chain, can deliver work that previously required a 5–10 person cross-functional team — at comparable quality and significantly higher velocity.

## Why This Matters

This is the core operational claim. The "For Hire" positioning (Arroyo Labs: "one designer, an AI crew, and the full pipeline") depends on this being true. If a solo practitioner with agents can only match 2–3x individual output (not team output), the positioning needs to downscale.

## Signals That Support It

- Arroyo Labs delivering client work at team-equivalent scope (internal case study)
- Investiture's crew skill successfully decomposing features into parallel agent tasks
- Fictioneer and Labrador built by a solo practitioner + AI crew
- Community members reporting similar leverage in their own projects

## Signals That Would Invalidate It

- Client work quality significantly lower than team-produced equivalents
- Coordination overhead of managing agents approaches coordination overhead of managing humans
- Architectural coherence degrades at scale (debt accumulates faster than agents can remediate)
- Solo practitioners burn out trying to maintain the pace
