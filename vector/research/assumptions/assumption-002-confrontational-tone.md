# Assumption 002: Confrontational Tone Attracts, Not Repels

**Status:** hypothesis
**Risk:** Medium (Impact: Medium × Confidence: Low)
**Personas:** All

## Statement

The manifesto's confrontational tone — "a 30-year indictment," "the handoff is dead," "hyperspecialization is for insects" — attracts the target audience (frustrated practitioners) rather than repelling them. The boldness signals authenticity and conviction.

## Why This Matters

Tone determines who self-selects into the community. If the confrontational framing attracts contrarians and repels pragmatists, the community will skew toward people who enjoy arguing philosophy rather than people who want to ship. The manifesto needs to feel like a rallying cry, not a rant.

## Signals That Support It

- High engagement / share rate on the manifesto page
- Organic social sharing with commentary like "finally someone said it"
- Sign-up conversions from manifesto visitors
- Builders and leaders (not just students) joining the community

## Signals That Would Invalidate It

- Bounce rate on manifesto page higher than site average
- Social commentary skewing toward "this is arrogant" or "who is this for"
- Enterprise leads citing tone as a barrier
- Community skews toward early-career / student rather than practitioners
